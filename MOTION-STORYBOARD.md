# Motion Storyboard

## Hero
- 0–33% scroll: brand statement; image begins at 112% scale.
- 33–66% scroll: business model statement; image zooms toward 107%; category line appears.
- 66–100% scroll: conversion statement; image reaches 104%; real CTAs appear.

## Rules
- No interactive-looking element without an action.
- No overlapping cards on the primary product image.
- No more than 8% total zoom.
- Keep text stable while transitioning phases.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
