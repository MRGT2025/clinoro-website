A professional medical-equipment catalogue and RFQ experience. This is not an ecommerce storefront.

2. Clear business definition
3. Product categories
4. Featured products
5. Decision-support narrative
6. Services
7. Solutions
8. Audiences
9. RFQ process
10. Documentation
11. Final CTA

## Content governance
- Do not publish manufacturer relationships, years of experience, client counts, response times, certifications or geography coverage without written confirmation.
- Every CTA must link to a real page or action.
- Product pages must eventually include actual model, manufacturer, intended use, regulatory documentation, warranty, installation and consumables.
